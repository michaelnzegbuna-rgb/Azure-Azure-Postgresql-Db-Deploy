INSTRUCTIONS FOR SCREENSHOT SUBMISSIONS
========================================

Please take screenshots of your deployed Azure PostgreSQL database and save them under this folder to overwrite the placeholder files:

1. server-overview.png
   - Capture: The 'Overview' blade of your server in the Azure Portal.
   - Purpose: Verifies that your server 'psql-lab-olamileye' exists, is active (status 'Running'), and displays the correct region (West Europe).

2. firewall.png
   - Capture: The 'Networking' blade (Public access tab) of your server.
   - Purpose: Verifies the firewall rules, specifically showing your client IP allowed under the name 'AllowMyDevIP'.

3. private-endpoint.png
   - Capture: The 'Networking' blade (Private access tab) of your server.
   - Purpose: Verifies that the Private Endpoint ('pe-psql-lab') is successfully integrated with the VNet subnet ('snet-db') and Private DNS.

4. connection_proof.png
   - Capture: Your pgAdmin 4 browser tree or terminal showing psql.
   - Purpose: Verifies that you have successfully established a TLS connection (using sslmode=require) and can see/query the 'labdb' database schema.
